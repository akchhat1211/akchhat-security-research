## Research Areas

- **Active Directory & Windows Security** — Domain enumeration, privilege escalation, lateral movement, persistence, and domain compromise.
- **Initial Access** — Phishing, HTML smuggling, DLL sideloading, and execution techniques.
- **Endpoint Security & EDR** — Testing detection capabilities, evaluating security controls, and understanding telemetry gaps.
- **Persistence** — Exploring Windows persistence mechanisms and their detection opportunities.
- **Red Team Operations** — Adversary techniques, operational security, and attack-chain development.

## How I Approach Research

I prefer hands-on testing over simply reproducing commands from existing guides.

My usual approach is to understand the technique, reproduce it in a controlled environment, observe its behaviour, examine the available telemetry, and document what I learn.

Not every experiment produces the expected result. Those observations are part of the research too.
