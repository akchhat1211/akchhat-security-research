# Akchhat's Security Writeups

I specialize in offensive Active Directory security, adversary simulation, and hands-on penetration testing. My writeups aren't step-by-step command dumps, they cover the reasoning behind each move, what's actually happening under the hood, and why it matters from a defensive perspective too.

Most of what I know didn't come from a syllabus. It came from building labs, breaking them, rebuilding them, and doing it again.

***

### What I Work On

**Active Directory Offensive Security** This is where most of my time goes. Full attack chains, enumeration, Kerberos abuse, delegation attacks, ACL exploitation, ADCS ESC chains, forest trust abuse. I've run these paths across home labs and practice environments enough times that the methodology is second nature, not a checklist.

**Red Team Operations** Adversary simulation built around OPSEC & C2 infrastructure, payload staging, EDR evasion, lateral movement that doesn't immediately announce itself. I work across Cobalt Strike, Adaptix, and Sliver depending on what the situation calls for.

**Security Research** Vulnerability analysis and exploit methodology, written for people who want to understand what's actually broken and why, not just run a PoC and move on.
